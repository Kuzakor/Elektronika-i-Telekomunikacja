## Reading: the noble gases

The noble gases (helium, neon, argon, krypton, xenon and radon) form group 18 of the periodic table. Their outer electron shell is full, so they hardly ever react with other elements. Argon makes up almost 1% of the air and is used to fill light bulbs.
