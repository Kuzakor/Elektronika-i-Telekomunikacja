- [[Jednostka urojona (i)]]
$$
z^n =|z|^ne^{in\phi} =|z|^n(\cos n\phi + i\sin n\phi)
$$
# Przykład
$$
\left(\frac{-2-2i}{-1+\sqrt{ 3 }i} \right)^{133} = \space?
$$
$$
z_{1} = -2-2i = 2\sqrt{ 2 } \space e^{i\frac{\pi}{4}}
$$
$$
z_{2}=-1+\sqrt{ 3 }i = 2e^{i \frac{2\pi}{3}}
$$
$$
\left(\frac{-2-2i}{-1+\sqrt{ 3 }i} \right)^{133} = \left(\frac{2\sqrt{ 2 } \space e^{i\frac{\pi}{4}}}{2e^{i \frac{2\pi}{3}}} \right) = (\sqrt{ 2 }e^{i \frac{7\pi}{12}})^{133} =  2^{66}\sqrt{ 2 } e^{i \frac{932}{12}\pi}
$$
